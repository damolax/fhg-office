My story first

I did not start from where I am today.

I am Olalekan Oyekunle. I started this journey as a student who wanted a better financial direction, better tools and more control over what my future could look like.

Where I started

My first result took patience.

I did not join because I already had everything. At one point, even getting a better phone mattered to me. I was a student trying to find a practical way to create income and move forward.

It took me 1 month and 23 days to make my first dollar through the business, and that first payment was $70. Since then, I have made far more than that and used the journey to improve my skills, tools, confidence and business thinking.

About FHG’T

A community built around skills, business, health and growth.

FHG’T brings together people who want to learn relevant digital skills, create active income, improve their wellbeing, build a network marketing business and grow with a community instead of trying to figure everything out alone.

The community has grown to more than 100,000 active members, with at least 20,000 people who have recorded strong personal progress in different ways, from phones and laptops to bikes, cars, family support, better living conditions and houses.

Among people who commit seriously to the process, the first $200 milestone often comes around the 5-month mark. Some reach it in about 2 months, while others may take closer to 1 year.
