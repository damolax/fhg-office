My story

I know what it feels like to want more and not know where to start.

I’m Olalekan Oyekunle, a Mechanical Engineering student at Obafemi Awolowo University.

01
Before the business

I was simply looking for a way forward.

I was a student trying to survive, learn something valuable and create income for myself.

I had started learning Android App Development because that was the skill I was interested in at the time. But one lesson I later learnt is that business does not always begin with your favourite skill. Sometimes the smarter starting point is the skill the market is already buying.

The problem for me then was simple: data was expensive, and eventually my phone spoiled too.

At that point, I needed a new phone badly. Not for status. I needed something I could use to keep learning, building and moving forward.

02
My turning point

I joined because I wanted to raise money for a phone.

Mr. Lawal Sheriffdeen introduced me to the business.

I did not start with some huge dream of becoming a business leader. My first goal was much smaller: learn the system, make money and get a better phone. I also had to become flexible about what I learnt first. The focus became less about “What skill do I like most?” and more about “What can I learn now that people are already paying for?”

That simple reason was enough to make me start.

My starting goal Get a better phone. Sometimes one practical need is enough to begin a much bigger journey.
The first proof

1 month, 23 days. Then $70.

It took me 1 month and 23 days to make my first dollar through the business. My first payment was $70.

That payment mattered because it changed the question in my mind from “Can this work?” to “How far can I take this?”

Why I keep building

I do not want to win alone.

For me, real success is better when the people around you are also growing.

I want to build a community of people who can earn, improve their lives and enjoy bigger experiences together. That community is built physically, through real meetings, mentorship and people working together.

That is why helping people learn useful skills and build their own income matters to me. The goal is not just to show somebody what I have done. It is to help them create a story they can be proud of too.

About FHG’T

My team is part of something much bigger.

FHG’T combines digital skills, active income, wellbeing and network marketing. The business itself is physical: mentorship, meetings, training support and team building happen at our location. The digital skills are what allow members to serve clients and earn online. Because we are business builders, we often guide beginners into top-selling skills first.

The community has grown to over 100,000 active members, with people at very different stages of life and progress.

A common early milestone we see among committed beginners is the first $200, often around the 5-month mark. Some move faster. Some take longer.
