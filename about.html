My story

I needed a way forward. FHG gave me a place to start.

I’m Olalekan Oyekunle. I started when I had almost no direction. This is the story of how learning a skill, finding people who believed in the process, and doing the work began changing things for me.

Before FHG

I was looking for a way forward.

I was still in school, but my attempt to learn Android development had stalled. Some mornings I would go to the Mosque, return home and wonder what I could do to change my situation.

01

I had tried to find my own way before.

A friend once told me Android developers could make around $90,000 per year. That number caught my attention immediately.

I asked him how long it would take me to know Android Development very well. He said about 3 years. I asked whether it was possible to learn faster. He said yes, because some people had done it. So I started.

02

Then everything stopped.

I was learning with my PC, but at one point my phone got spoilt. I had no proper data connection, no money and no practical way to continue the Android Development journey.

I was still in school, but I became so frustrated with myself that I could hardly even go for classes. At one point, I had already made up my mind that I would collect a leave of absence from school and go and look for something I could do to make money.

03

The turning point happened at the Mosque.

One day, the person who later introduced the opportunity to me came to the same Mosque. I saw him working on Fiverr.

What surprised me was not only that he was making money through Fiverr. I could not believe that somebody I could meet physically actually understood how the platform worked. At the time, Fiverr looked extremely difficult to me.

I went to meet him. He told me I could learn it too and asked me to come to their office.

04

Before I even started, I could picture my first new phone.

In the two weeks before I started the business, I was already imagining what I would buy once I began earning. A phone was at the top of my list because of what I wanted to learn and do. Mine had stopped working while I was trying to learn Android development, so getting another phone meant more to me than just owning something new.

I had not earned anything yet. I just had a picture in my mind of what I wanted to work towards.

05

I came to the office and began with what the market needed.

When I started, my first skill was Email Marketing. That was what my mentors suggested because businesses were already paying for it. The digital skills training was free; the real work for me was learning to use the skill and figuring out how to serve clients.

It was not about asking, “What do I feel like learning?” It was about starting with something the market was already buying. That market-first thinking is still how we help people get started today.

My first proof

1 month, 23 days. Then $70.

I thought making money would be easy at first. It wasn't. It took me 1 month and 23 days after I started to earn my first $70.

That first result changed how I saw the system. There was no fixed monthly salary waiting for me; the opportunity was to learn skills and apply them myself. Looking back at the skills I've learned, the projects I've worked on and the people I've helped, I can say the effort was worth it.

What happened next

I did not stop at one skill.

I have now made money through more than 15 different skills.

What this business changed for me

The results became bigger than the phone I originally needed.

Since joining, I have personally bought more than five phones, bought PCs, and I have also helped other people achieve phones of their own.

Across my active team, more than 25 people now have their own PCs to work with, courtesy of the business.

I have a 2-bedroom apartment in Ikorodu and another 2-bedroom apartment in Ile-Ife, courtesy of the business.

I sponsored myself fully through school without support from home. I also send money home to my Mum, Dad, siblings and people I love.

What means even more to me is that I now have people in my team doing the same thing I am doing and creating their own progress too.

Why I keep doing this

Our business is a business of helping people.

I do not want this story to stop with me.

I have helped people get phones. People in my team are getting PCs, projects and income opportunities of their own.

I look forward to helping more people get started, learn the right skills, build their own results and eventually help somebody else too. The best part of this system, to me, is that you can learn to do the work yourself instead of waiting for somebody to pay you a monthly salary.
