My story

I did not start with a plan. I started because I needed a way forward.

I’m Olalekan Oyekunle. What I have today came from a period when I had almost no direction at all.

Read My Story Watch the explanation ↗
Before FHG

There was a time I was doing almost nothing.

I would wake up early in the morning, go to the Mosque, come back, and have no clear mission for the day.

01

I had tried to find my own way before.

A friend once told me Android developers could make around $90,000 per year. That number caught my attention immediately.

I asked him how long it would take me to know Android Development very well. He said about 3 years. I asked whether it was possible to learn faster. He said yes, because some people had done it. So I started.

02

Then everything stopped.

I was learning with my PC, but at one point my phone got spoilt. I had no proper data connection, no money and no practical way to continue the Android Development journey.

I was still in school, but I became so frustrated that I was barely going to classes. I even told myself I would collect a leave of absence from school and go and find something I could do to make money.

03

The turning point happened at the Mosque.

One day, the person who later introduced the opportunity to me came to the same Mosque. I saw him working on Fiverr.

What surprised me was not only that he was making money through Fiverr. I could not believe that somebody I could meet physically actually understood how the platform worked. At the time, Fiverr looked extremely difficult to me.

I approached him. He told me I could learn it too and asked me to come to their office.

04

I came to the office and started from what was selling.

My first skill was Email Marketing, because that was what they suggested I should start with. It was not about asking, “What do I feel like learning?” It was about starting with something the market was already paying for.

That same market-first thinking is still how we help people today.

My first proof

1 month, 23 days. Then $70.

It took me 1 month and 23 days to make my first money through the business. My first payment was $70.

That first result changed my thinking completely. Since then, I have kept learning, taking projects and expanding what I can do.
