01 / The beginning

Why Mr Jagunmolu Abd Fatai started FHG.

Founder

Mr Jagunmolu Abd Fatai

A determination to succeed became the starting point of a different kind of system.

01

He believed in network marketing.

Mr Jagunmolu wanted to build the kind of life he pictured for himself. He believed network marketing could help him get there, so he decided to try it. But determination alone was not enough. He still needed to know how to sell the products he had purchased, and that part was difficult for him.

02

Then came a different question.

He knew there were other people in the same position: people who saw potential in the business, but struggled to sell. What if there were a system people could actually follow? What if selling your network marketing products were a choice rather than the only way you could support yourself?

03

A skill first. Learn how to monetize it next.

Mr Jagunmolu saw a better sequence. First, help people learn a marketable digital skill. Then give them practical tools and guidance to monetize that skill: build a portfolio, find clients, present an offer and deliver projects that pay. The goal was to establish active income before beginning the network marketing journey.

04

Then build the network marketing business.

That sequence gave birth to FHG. Once a member has learned to earn from a skill and the tools that support it, they can begin building their network marketing business from their own active income. Purchasing wellness supplements for personal use and selling them do not have to be the same thing. Product sales can be a choice rather than the only source of money needed to keep building the business.

04 / The system

First the skill. Then the income. Then the business.

FHG's skills-first approach is about order. Learn a valuable skill through free training. Use freelancing platforms, client-finding tools and practical guidance to monetize it and strengthen your active income. After you have started earning from that skill, you can begin the network marketing journey with income you have worked to create.

01Learn a marketable skill

Build competence in a service clients need.

02Monetize it with the right tools

Use portfolios, freelance platforms and client-acquisition tools to turn the skill into active income.

03Start your network marketing journey

After establishing earnings from your skill, use your active income and team guidance to begin building the network marketing business.
