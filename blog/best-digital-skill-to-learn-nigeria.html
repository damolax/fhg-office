Which Digital Skill Should You Learn First in Nigeria?

The “best” skill is not simply the one people are talking about this week. A better choice fits the tools you can access, the problems you enjoy solving and the kind of practice you can complete consistently.

By Oyekunle Olalekan Musodiq• October 20, 2026

Start with the problem you want to solve

If you enjoy arranging information into clear pages, website design may be a good fit. If you think visually and enjoy layout, try graphic design. If you are comfortable writing and organising messages, email marketing could suit you. If you are detail-oriented, project support and research may be useful starting points.

Some digital skills have lower equipment barriers for learning than others. However, a low barrier to learning does not necessarily mean it is easy to earn. Clients still need good work, responsiveness and reliability.

Use a simple decision score

Give each area a score from 1 (difficult or weak) to 5 (strong). Compare two or three skills. The table shows how someone with a limited laptop schedule might think through the decision; your own scores will differ.

The score is a personal learning decision tool, not a market-research ranking. It suggests graphics might be a sensible first skill for that hypothetical learner, but not necessarily the best long-term career.

How to check whether a skill is marketable

Look at what businesses genuinely need. Read freelance briefs for recurring problems, not only the job titles. Ask local entrepreneurs what they struggle with. Compare the expected result with your current ability and the time it would take you to practise.

For instance, a small business owner might not search for “email marketing specialist.” They might say, “Customers sign up but never hear from us.” Understanding that problem helps you position a focused service such as a welcome sequence.

A two-week trial before you commit

If you dislike the work after trying honestly, choosing another direction is sensible. If you enjoy it but find it hard, that may mean you need more practice rather than an immediate change.
