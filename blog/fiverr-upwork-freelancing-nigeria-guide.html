Fiverr vs Upwork in Nigeria: Which Platform Should a Beginner Choose?

People often ask, “Should I start with Fiverr or Upwork?” The better question is whether you have a service worth buying, enough examples to demonstrate it and time to respond professionally. Then the platforms become useful channels, not magic income buttons.

By Oyekunle Olalekan Musodiq• October 20, 2026

First, understand what each platform is designed to do

Both marketplaces help clients connect with freelancers. On Fiverr, freelancers generally present packaged services called Gigs that buyers can browse. On Upwork, freelancers can browse client job posts and submit tailored proposals; some clients also discover freelancers through profiles and other marketplace features.

Neither platform is guaranteed to send clients. Both update their policies, payment options and fees periodically, so check the official help centres before signing up or quoting a price.

What do clients want to know before ordering?

Imagine you offer WordPress websites. The buyer probably doesn't care how many tutorials you've watched. They want to know whether their website will load properly, display well on a phone, explain their business and include a way to contact them.

A strong offer answers five questions: Who is it for? What is included? What is excluded? How long does it take? What do you need from the client?

Weak descriptionMore useful positioning
“I design any kind of website”“I build a responsive five-page WordPress website for local service businesses.”
“I'm expert in graphics”“I create five branded promotional graphics with editable source files.”
“I do email marketing”“I set up a three-email welcome sequence and test the signup flow.”

A practical setup checklist

Choose one service. Produce two or three original samples. Use a professional-looking, accurate profile photo and description. State the deliverables clearly. Learn how the marketplace handles messages, milestones, payments and disputes. Never buy fake reviews or pretend to have worked for companies you haven't.

On Upwork, focus your first proposals on projects you can genuinely deliver. Explain what you understood about the client's problem, outline a small plan and include a relevant sample. On Fiverr, focus on a narrow Gig and realistic packages rather than stuffing a title with unrelated services.

What should you consider about pricing?

Calculate your likely time, operating costs, platform fees and revision risk. The numbers below are budgeting examples, not typical earnings, promised rates or current platform-fee quotes.

A higher headline price is not always more profitable. Complex revisions, subscriptions, mobile data, payment conversion and time spent winning the client can reduce the amount you keep.

What about being paid in foreign currency?

International clients may pay in foreign currencies through supported platform systems, but the availability of withdrawal methods depends on the platform, your account, country, identification and current payment partners. Don't assume a method works before reading its latest official documentation. Keep your work and payments within each platform's rules.

You can review official Fiverr guidance and Upwork's new-freelancer guidance.
