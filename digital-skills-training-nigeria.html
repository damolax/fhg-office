Free Digital Skills Training in Nigeria for Beginners

Whether you are a student, graduate, jobseeker, working professional or starting over, you can explore free digital skills training through Faitheroic Global Team (FHG). Start by learning a useful digital skill, learn how to present your work to clients, and ask about physical mentorship options in your preferred Nigerian state or city. You do not need to know Oyekunle Olalekan Musodiq or FHG before taking the first step.

How the training works

Learn first. Practise. Find clients.

Common questions

Before you join

Is the digital skills training really free?

Yes. The digital skills instruction described on this site is free. The separate network marketing business is a later step; ask about any participation conditions before making a commitment.

Can I join without a technical background?

Yes. Beginners can ask about starting with a suitable, practical skill.

Do you guarantee a freelancing job or income?

No. Training and guidance are offered, but paid projects depend on client demand and your ability to win and deliver work.

Can I enquire from any Nigerian state?

Yes. Tell us your state, city and preferred area through the form. We will discuss what guidance and physical office options may be available in your area.
