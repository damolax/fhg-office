Straight answers

Before you join, know what it is.

Do I need to already have a skill?

No. You can start from zero. What matters is whether you are ready to learn and practise.

Is this a salary job?

No. It is a skill and business opportunity. You are learning how to create value, earn actively and build a business.

Will I make money immediately?

No promise like that. Some people move faster than others. Your result depends on what you learn, how well you practise and how consistently you work.

Can I do this while in school or working?

Yes, many people start part-time. You still need to manage your time seriously.

Ready to hear the full explanation?

You do not need to know everything before you start.
