My story

I did not start from where I am today.

I started as a student looking for a better way to create income and improve my situation. My first dollar through this business did not come overnight. It took me 1 month and 23 days to make it, and that first payment was $70. I have made far more than that since then.

That first $70 meant something because it proved to me that the process could work when I stayed with it. From there, the goal became bigger: improve my skills, get better tools, work with more clients and build something that could keep growing.

Your own first win may look completely different from mine, and that is fine. What matters is that you are moving forward from where you started.

About FHG’T

Different people. Different starting points. Different journeys.

FHG’T is a large community built around digital skills, entrepreneurship, network marketing, personal development and helping people create better options for themselves.

FHG’T has grown into a community of more than 100,000 active members, with at least 20,000 people who have recorded strong personal progress in different ways, including buying phones, laptops, MacBooks, bikes and cars, supporting family needs, improving their living conditions and, for some, building houses.

From what we commonly see among people who commit seriously to the process, reaching the first $200 milestone often happens around the 5-month mark. Some get there in about 2 months, while others may take closer to 1 year. The journey is personal, but the point is to keep building until your own result starts showing.

Straight answers

Before you join, know what it is.

Do I need to already have a skill?

No. You can start from zero. What matters is whether you are ready to learn and practise.

Is this a salary job?

No. It is a skill and business opportunity. You are learning how to create value, earn actively and build a business.

How does the commission work?

The business has a commission structure. As you build activity, help people get started and grow your network, qualifying business volume is rewarded through the compensation plan.

Can I do this while in school or working?

Yes, many people start part-time. You still need to manage your time seriously.

Ready to hear the full explanation?

You do not need to know everything before you start.
