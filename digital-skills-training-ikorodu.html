What can I learn?

Start with skills people pay for.

Digital skills are useful whether you are a student, a jobseeker or already working. These are some of the practical topics introduced within the free training pathway.

How the training works

Learn first. Practise. Find clients.

01

Tell us your starting point

Describe your level, the skill you want to learn and the area of Lagos you can visit. A team member can explain the available in-person guidance.

02

Build a useful digital skill

Start with free instruction, practise on examples and learn to present your work clearly. You may begin learning with a smartphone; access to a laptop becomes helpful for many paid projects.

03

Understand freelancing

Explore how to prepare a portfolio, describe a service and reach potential clients through platforms such as Fiverr or Upwork and through direct outreach.

04

Build your own progress

Apply what you learn to real projects. Paid work depends on your skills, effort, demand and client decisions; it is never automatic.

Common questions

Before you join

Is the digital skills training really free?

Yes. The digital skills instruction described on this site is free. The separate network marketing business is a later step; ask about any participation conditions before making a commitment.

Can I join without a technical background?

Yes. Beginners can ask about starting with a suitable, practical skill.

Do you guarantee a freelancing job or income?

No. Training and guidance are offered, but paid projects depend on client demand and your ability to win and deliver work.

Can someone outside Ikorodu ask about training?

Yes. Tell us your preferred area or city through the contact form and we can discuss available physical office options.
